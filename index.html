For Teachers Who Are Running on Coffee

  • Pairs & Small Groups

    Because asking 30 students to work independently while you desperately try to find the HDMI cable is a completely reasonable classroom strategy. These activities work well in pairs or small groups.

  • Projector-Proof-ish

    Big text. High contrast. Minimal nonsense. Designed to remain readable even when your classroom projector looks like it was purchased during the Industrial Revolution.

  • No Accounts. No Tracking. No Drama.

    Students don't need to create an account, remember another password, or receive a suspicious email saying “Your account is ready!” Their answers stay in the page and disappear when the tab closes. Just like their motivation approximately 17 minutes into class.

Want to Add Something?

Go ahead. Make the teacher internet slightly less chaotic. These activities are just plain HTML files, so adding one is surprisingly painless. No ancient programming rituals required.

  1. 1. Create the folder

    Add my-activity/index.html to the repository. Try to give it a useful name. Future-you will thank present-you. Probably.

  2. 2. Add the resource

    Add the title, link, subject, level and a short description to assets/js/resources.js. The website will do the boring part for you. Because that's what computers are for.

  3. 3. Commit & Open a PR

    Push your changes and open a pull request. GitHub Pages will rebuild the site automatically while you pretend you understand everything that just happened.

⚠️ Dear Student: Read This.

Congratulations. You found the teacher's website.

Yes, you're here to learn. No, clicking random buttons until class ends does not count.

About your phone:
Your phone will survive five minutes without you. TikTok will still be there. The group chat will still be chaotic. Put it down.

About AI:
AI can help you learn. AI can also do your entire assignment while you sit there doing absolutely nothing.

If AI does everything for you, the AI gets smarter and you get... the same grade with less knowledge. Amazing strategy.

Use AI to explain things. Use it to practice. Use it to check your work. Don't use it as your replacement brain. You only get one.

And please don't submit an essay that sounds like a university professor wrote it when yesterday you wrote: “Teacher i dont understand lol.”

We know.

Make mistakes. That's literally how learning works. Nobody expects perfect English. We do expect you to try.

And if the activity says: “Work with a partner,” please don't spend ten minutes deciding who is going to do absolutely nothing.

Now put your phone away and do the activity.

Your teacher actually cares about you. Annoyingly, that's why they're making you learn.
